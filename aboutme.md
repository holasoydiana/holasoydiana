I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Bristol, working on the project "Women Walking in Latin America" funded by the Leverhulme Trust.  
I recently finished my PhD at the Interdisciplinary Global Development Centre at the University of York. My thesis focused on public transportation in Saltillo through a gender perspective. My research interests include **the intersection of care work and mobility**, as well as **sustainable active travel and public policy research for walkable cities**. I have been awarded multiple scholarships throughout my professional development, such as the Sir Richard Stapley Educational Trust, the Chevening Awards (Pacific Alliance) and the Doctoral Scholarships for Postgraduate Students Abroad by CONACyT (SECIHTI) in Mexico. Those awards have made my pursuits in higher education possible. 

My doctoral research project applied a mixed-method approach to urban audits and identified infrastructure gaps in relation to women's perceptions of vulnerability and safety. I focused on their lived experiences and engaged with a feminist-informed approach to _sentipensar_. My latest project will focus on the mobility of women in Chile, Mexico, and Brazil who live in rural areas and dynamically commute to urban centres. I am particularly interested in their _caminares_ (walks) and the challenges they face as they swing back and forth, and how these journeys reconfigure their sense of belonging and their lived experiences throughout space. 
I am passionate about exploring cities by walking and cycling, and I advocate for walkable communities through my activism. I am particularly interested in women's right to the city and how policy can improve their urban experiences.

I earned my bachelor’s degree in Architecture from Tecnológico de Monterrey in Mexico and completed my MSc. in International Planning and Urban Design at Cardiff University. 
I am passionate about **exploring creative and collaborative outlets** when it comes to researching and envisioning **gender-conscious cities**. My work has been published in journals like Environment and Planning C: Politics and Space; Gender, Place, and Culture; and the Journal of Gender Studies and you can access my work on the tab [research papers.](researchpapers.md)
